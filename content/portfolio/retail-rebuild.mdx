## Challenge

Horizon Retail was running on a monolithic legacy stack that made releases slow and mobile performance poor. Customers abandoned carts at twice the industry average.

## Approach

We migrated the storefront to a headless architecture, introduced a React-based PWA for checkout, and built an internal inventory dashboard that gave the operations team real-time visibility.

## Results

Mobile conversion jumped by over two-thirds, cart abandonment dropped sharply, and the platform has maintained near-perfect uptime since launch.
