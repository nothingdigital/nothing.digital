## Challenge

Launch a credible agency site that generates scoping calls without shipping dark patterns, fabricated case studies, or unfinished protocol experiments.

## Approach

We kept the public surface thin: services, honest pricing ranges, contact + Calendly, newsletter, and blog. Lead capture writes to Supabase and emails the team via Resend. Owner `/admin` handles inbox triage, clients, invoices, assets, and work. Analytics is Umami behind cookie consent; uptime is UptimeRobot.

## Outcome

The site is live at [nothing.digital](https://nothing.digital). Portfolio slots stay empty until real Founding Client work ships. Growth work now is content cadence, Listmonk drips, and two invite-only Founding Client engagements — not more product surface.
