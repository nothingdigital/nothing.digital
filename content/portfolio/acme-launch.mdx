## Challenge

Acme Co. needed to stand out in a saturated market with a launch that felt premium but approachable. Their existing site was slow, their email list was underused, and the checkout flow leaked conversions.

## Approach

We rebuilt the storefront around speed and clarity: a custom Next.js frontend, optimized product pages, and a post-purchase email sequence designed to turn first-time buyers into repeat customers.

## Results

The campaign outperformed forecasts across every channel. Page load times dropped under one second, email revenue increased by six figures in the first quarter, and the conversion rate lifted by more than 40%.
