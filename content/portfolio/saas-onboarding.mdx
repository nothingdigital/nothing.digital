## Challenge

CloudSync's product was powerful but intimidating. New users dropped off during setup, and activation rates lagged behind the category benchmark.

## Approach

We designed a guided onboarding flow with progressive profiling, interactive tutorials, and contextual emails triggered by user behavior. Every step was instrumented so the team could iterate quickly.

## Results

Activation rose by more than half, the time to first value fell by 40%, and the improved onboarding directly contributed to an 18% reduction in churn.
