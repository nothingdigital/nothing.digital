## Challenge

The previous site buried locations and phone numbers, loaded slowly on mobile, and sent appointment intent into a generic contact form that the front desk struggled to triage.

## Approach

We rebuilt the information architecture around locations and services, tightened Core Web Vitals, and wired a short booking-intent form that routes by clinic. A light email sequence follows incomplete inquiries.

## Outcome

Organic inquiries and completed booking forms rose within the first quarter. Page speed stopped being a complaint from staff sharing links on their phones.
