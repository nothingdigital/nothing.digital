## Challenge

Dispatchers tracked shipments across email threads and shared sheets. Status lagged reality, and escalation rules lived in people's heads.

## Approach

We mapped the real workflow, then shipped a read-heavy dashboard with role-based views, SLA timers, and integrations to the systems they already trusted. Weekly demos kept scope honest.

## Outcome

The ops team reclaimed roughly two workdays a week of manual reconciliation and catches SLA risk earlier in the cycle.
