## Challenge

The studio had a growing list and a strong offer, but sends were ad hoc. There was no welcome path, no proof sequence, and no clear handoff to a scoping call.

## Approach

We audited the list, set double opt-in and segments, and built a three-email welcome drip plus a monthly dispatch tied to the site's case studies and pricing ranges.

## Outcome

Open rates stabilized above industry baselines for B2B services, and discovery-call bookings from email rose without discounting the offer.
