Email automation works best when it feels personal. The goal is not to send more messages, but to send the right message at the right time.

## Map the journey

Before writing a single email, map the customer journey. Identify the moments where a timely message adds value: welcome, onboarding, abandoned cart, post-purchase, re-engagement.

## Segment by behavior

Demographics matter, but behavior matters more. Segment users by what they have done, not just who they are. A user who opened three pricing pages needs a different message than one who only reads blog posts.

## Write like a human

Automation should not sound robotic. Use plain language, one clear call to action, and a tone that matches your brand. Test subject lines, send times, and content regularly.

Done well, email automation builds relationships at scale.
