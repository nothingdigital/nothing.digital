# Founding Client Package — Design Spec

**Date:** 2026-08-06  
**Status:** Approved  
**Owner:** Nothing.Digital (The Business of Nothing LLC)

## Purpose

Private offer for **exactly two** portfolio clients: a discounted custom website build plus **12 months** of monitoring and light upkeep, then a paid care plan (or clean handoff). Separate from the free mother-in-law build.

Public site pricing stays quote-after-scoping. This package is **offline / invite-only** — not a published rate card.

## Positioning

> You’re a Founding Client: a real custom site at roughly half rate, with 12 months of monitoring & light upkeep included. After that, a simple monthly care plan — or we hand everything off clean.

Brand fit: senior studio, fixed scope, fixed fee, “Built on time. Built to last.”

## Pricing

| Item             | Amount                                                    |
| ---------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| Fixed build fee  | **$2,500**                                                |
| Year-1 care      | **Included** (12 months from launch)                      |
| Care (year 2+)   | **$99/mo**                                                |
| Care + (year 2+) | **$149/mo** (~2 hrs tweaks/mo)                            |
| Pause / handoff  | **$0** — site stays; monitoring stops; client owns assets |

**Payment:** 50% on signing / before kickoff · 50% at launch. Care window starts at launch.

**Internal reference:** normal marketing sites ballpark $5K–$15K (`docs/growth-tactics.md`). Founding rate is intentional portfolio discount, not a permanent SKU.

## Who it fits

One package flexes across:

- Local service businesses (brochure + contact)
- Light booking / menu / catalog (via fixed add-ons)
- Personal brand / consultants / coaches

## Build scope ($2,500)

### In scope

- Custom marketing site, typically **5–7 pages** (Home, About, Services/Offer, Contact, plus 1–2 as needed)
- Mobile-responsive, performance-minded, on-brand
- Contact form → client (or studio) email
- Basic SEO (titles, meta, sitemap, analytics)
- Domain connect + launch on studio stack
- **2 revision rounds** after first full draft
- Timeline: **7–14 days after client content is in** (copy, photos, brand bits)

### Fixed add-ons (quoted separately, not free)

- Online booking embed
- Menu / catalog / simple product list
- Blog or resources section
- Pages beyond 7

### Out of scope (unless Change Order / add-on)

- Ecommerce checkout / payments
- Custom app / member portal
- Heavy copywriting from scratch (client supplies; studio may polish)
- Logo / brand system from zero
- Redesigns or new features after launch (care ≠ new build)

## Year-1 care (included)

- Uptime monitoring + down alerts
- SSL / domain / hosting health checks
- Dependency & security patches as needed
- Light content tweaks (copy, images, hours, menu items): **~1 hour/mo**, bank up to ~2 hrs
- Short monthly “site still healthy” email

**Not in care:** redesigns, new pages/features, SEO campaigns/ads, on-demand content creation beyond light tweaks.

## Year-2 renewal

At **month 11**, send renewal options (Care / Care+ / Pause).  
Cancel → clean handoff (repo/access, DNS notes, update how-to). No hostage hosting.

## Studio non-negotiables

- Portfolio + case study rights (name, screenshots, short story; anonymize only if client insists)
- One short testimonial after launch
- Cap: **2 Founding Clients** total
- Content delay ≥ 2 weeks → timeline pauses (day-for-day)
