## 1. Ownership

Client owns ESP account, billing, domains, sender identity, lists, and data. Studio gets **role-based access** only — not account/billing/sender-of-record unless expressly agreed (not recommended). After termination + amounts due, Studio access ends; reasonable handoff help for **5 business days**.

ESP: **[Klaviyo / HubSpot / Mailchimp / ConvertKit / other]**

## 2. Scope

Unless the SOW says otherwise: deliverables as listed in SOW; **2** revision rounds per major deliverable; one primary ESP; post-launch optimization excluded unless retainer SOW.

## 3. Compliance

**Client:** lawful collection/consent; privacy notices and marketing claims; suppression across Client systems; CAN-SPAM sender identity/address; CASL/other regimes if sending there; SMS/TCPA only if separately scoped.

**Studio:** agreed footer/unsubscribe/technical settings; configure SOW automations/templates; flag obvious implementation issues noticed during work. Not legal advice; does not verify historical consent for the whole list.

## 4. Deliverability

Commercially reasonable practices only — **no guarantee** of inbox placement, rates, revenue, sender score, or platform approval. Client bears list quality, sending history, domain reputation, purchased/scraped lists, and content-claim risk. Studio may pause unsafe/unlawful-looking sends until Client remediates. Launch may require auth (SPF/DKIM/DMARC), suppression import, and hygiene steps in the SOW.

## 5. Data and IP

Identifiable subscriber access → DPA. Studio uses data only for the SOW; deletes/returns Studio-held copies on request after (legal retention excepted). Custom templates/docs transfer on full payment per MSA; ESP features under ESP terms; Studio keeps general playbooks/know-how.
