## 2. Purpose

This MSA sets general terms for Studio’s professional services. Each engagement is a Statement of Work or similar order (“SOW”). If an SOW conflicts with this MSA, the SOW controls for that engagement only. Website Terms and Privacy govern site use only — they do not replace this MSA or any SOW.

## 3. Services

Studio performs the services in each SOW (e.g. websites, software, apps, email, AI, related consulting). Education/tutoring uses the separate Education Client Services Agreement unless an SOW expressly includes it.

## 4. Statements of Work

Each SOW states scope, deliverables, exclusions, timeline, Client dependencies, fees, payment, acceptance, assumptions, and third-party tools. Work starts only after both parties sign the SOW and any required deposit is received (e-sign OK).

## 5. Changes

Scope, fee, timeline, or assumption changes need a signed Change Order before Studio does the changed work. Studio may pause related work until then.

## 6. Fees and payment

1. Fees are as stated in the SOW; fixed for the documented scope unless stated otherwise.
2. Invoices due as stated in the SOW (default: deposits on receipt; otherwise Net 15).
3. Late amounts may accrue 1.5% per month or the max rate allowed by law, whichever is less.
4. Studio may suspend work for invoices more than **14 days** past due after written notice.
5. Third-party costs (hosting, domains, ESP, stores, model/API usage, licenses, stock, ads, travel) are Client’s unless the SOW says Studio will purchase and pass them through. Unlisted pass-throughs need prior written approval.
6. Deposits are **non-refundable** once work on that SOW has started, except as required by law or agreed in writing.

## 7. Client responsibilities

Client will provide timely access, content, credentials, decisions, and feedback; warrant rights to materials/data it supplies; name one primary approver; and meet SOW dependencies. Client delays may extend timelines and may trigger Change Order fees.

## 8. Acceptance

Unless the SOW says otherwise: Studio delivers a milestone → Client has **5 business days** to accept or list material SOW non-conformities in writing → silence is acceptance → Studio cures material SOW defects in a commercially reasonable time → out-of-scope requests are Change Orders, not rejection grounds.

## 9. Intellectual property

1. **Studio Materials.** Studio keeps pre-existing tools, frameworks, templates, libraries, general prompts, know-how, and methods. Client gets a non-exclusive license to use them only as embodied in paid deliverables.
2. **Work product.** On **full payment** for an SOW, Studio assigns bespoke deliverables created uniquely for Client under that SOW (excluding Studio Materials and third-party materials).
3. **Third-party.** OSS/vendor components stay under their licenses; Client handles ongoing compliance after handoff unless the SOW says otherwise.
4. **Portfolio.** Studio may show non-confidential work and Client’s name/logo unless Client objects in writing.

## 10. Confidentiality

Each party protects the other’s non-public information with reasonable care and uses it only to perform under this MSA. Exclusions: public, independently developed, or rightfully received from a third party. Survives **3 years** after termination (trade secrets: while they remain trade secrets).

## 11. Data and privacy

If Studio processes personal data for Client, parties will use a DPA as needed. Client warrants lawful rights/notices/consents for data it provides. Studio will not use Client confidential data to train Studio’s own general models or products.

## 12. Warranties and disclaimers

Studio warrants professional, workmanlike performance. Otherwise services/deliverables are **as is**. No warranty of third-party uptime, rankings, inbox placement, revenue, app-store approval, or AI/automation accuracy for high-risk use.

## 13. Limitation of liability

To the max extent permitted by law: no indirect/incidental/special/consequential/lost-profits damages; Studio’s total liability is limited to **fees paid under the SOW giving rise to the claim** in the **12 months** before the claim; limits do not apply to fraud, willful misconduct, or non-limitable liability.

## 14. Indemnity

Client indemnifies Studio for third-party claims from Client content, data, products/services, regulatory duties, or misuse of deliverables (except Studio willful misconduct).

Studio indemnifies Client for third-party U.S. copyright claims that Studio-created bespoke deliverables (excluding Client materials, licensed Studio Materials, third-party components, and AI model outputs) infringe, subject to prompt notice, Studio defense control, and Section 13.

## 15. Term and termination

Continues until terminated. Either party may terminate this MSA for convenience on **30 days’** written notice (open SOWs continue unless also terminated). Either may terminate an SOW or this MSA for uncured material breach after **10 days’** written notice. On termination, Client pays for work performed and approved expenses. IP, confidentiality, payment, liability, and indemnity survive.

## 16. Independent contractor

Studio is an independent contractor (no partnership/employment). Studio may use subcontractors and remains responsible for them.

## 17. Governing law and disputes

Alabama law. Venue: state or federal courts in **[COUNTY/CITY], Alabama**. Parties negotiate in good faith for **14 days** after written dispute notice before filing suit.

## 18. General

Email notice to the addresses above is enough if acknowledged or not bounced. No assignment without consent except to a successor in a merger/sale of substantially all assets. This MSA + SOWs + Change Orders + riders are the entire agreement for covered services. Amendments in writing signed. Severability; e-sign binding.
