# Founding Client — Pitch One-Pager

**Internal use.** Invite-only. Cap: **2 clients**.  
**Spec:** [Founding Client package design](../superpowers/specs/2026-08-06-founding-client-package-design.md)

---

## One-liner

You’re a Founding Client: a real custom site at roughly half rate, with **12 months of monitoring & light upkeep included**. After that, a simple monthly care plan — or we hand everything off clean.

---

## What’s included

|                 |                                                                                                            |
| --------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Build**       | Custom marketing site (typically 5–7 pages), mobile-ready, contact form, basic SEO, domain connect, launch |
| **Timeline**    | **7–14 days** after we have your copy, photos, and brand bits                                              |
| **Revisions**   | 2 rounds after the first full draft                                                                        |
| **Year 1 care** | Monitoring, SSL/hosting health, security patches, light content tweaks (~1 hr/mo), monthly health note     |
| **Investment**  | **$2,500** fixed (50% to start, 50% at launch)                                                             |

### Easy add-ons (quoted fixed)

Booking embed · menu/catalog · blog/resources · extra pages

### Not included

Ecommerce checkout · custom apps · full copywriting from scratch · brand-from-zero · redesigns after launch

---

## After year 1

| Option     | Price   | What happens                                            |
| ---------- | ------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Care**   | $99/mo  | Same monitoring + light tweaks                          |
| **Care +** | $149/mo | Care + priority tweaks (~2 hrs/mo)                      |
| **Pause**  | $0      | Site stays yours; we stop monitoring and hand off clean |

---

## What we ask in return

- Portfolio and case-study rights (screenshots + short story)
- One short testimonial after launch
- Content on time — if copy/photos stall 2+ weeks, the clock pauses

---

## Fit checklist (before you offer)

- [ ] Slot still open (max 2 Founding Clients)
- [ ] Marketing site / light flex — not a custom app or full shop
- [ ] Client can supply content (or pay for copy separately)
- [ ] They’re OK with portfolio/case study rights
- [ ] Not a free/family build (those stay outside this package)
